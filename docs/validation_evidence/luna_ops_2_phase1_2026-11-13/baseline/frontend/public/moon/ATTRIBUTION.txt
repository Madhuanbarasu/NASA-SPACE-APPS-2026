These images are optimized for aesthetics, not science (SVS). In LUNA-OPS they are imagery and visual relief
shading only: no elevation, illumination or other scientific value is derived from them. Latitudes beyond 70 deg are
filled by SVS with a lower-resolution LOLA albedo map.
