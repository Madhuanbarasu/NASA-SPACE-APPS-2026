A New View of the Lunar South Pole from LOLA
Michael Kenneth Barker
All TIF files are cloud-optimized GeoTiffs (
COG
s) with south polar stereographic X/Y coordinates in meters and in the MOON_ME reference frame of the JPL DE421 ephemeris.
Filename format: PREFIX_LAT_PIX_STEMS.TIF
where PREFIX gives the data type (LDEM, LDEC, LDSM, LDRM, or LPSR), LAT gives the latitude limit (80S, 83S, 75S or
60S), PIX gives the pixel scale in meters per pixel (e.g., 10MPP, 20MPP, 30MPP, 40MPP, 60MPP, etc.), and STEMS
is an additional identifier (e.g., ADJ, ADJ_ERR, ADJ_HILL, ADJ_EFFRES, ADJ_ROUGH_100M, etc.).
